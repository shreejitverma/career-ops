Project administration

  • Project code: All course projects should be completed INDIVIDUALLY. AI tools are allowed during
    implementation, but students must disclose their AI usage and provide the requested AI logs. Regardless of AI
    tool use, students are responsible for understanding all code they submit. We will use an autograder
    to grade the project for correctness and meeting the stated requirements and generate a raw score (R).



                                                          5
  • Project Interviews: Students have to sign up for a short 15-minute oral interview. The intent is to verify
    the student’s understanding of their submitted work. The interview score (m) will be between 0 and 1: 0 if
    the student shows no understanding of the submitted project; 1 if the student shows complete understanding.
    We will provide a high-level writeup of what you can expect during the project interview.
  • Project grade: The project grade will be:
                                                        R×m

  • Interview signup: Students are responsible for signing up for an interview slot by the announced deadline.
    Missing the interview without a valid documented reason will result in a zero for the project. Late by more
    than 5 minutes for the interview OR last-minute rescheduling within 24 hours of the signed-up interview slot
    will result in a 25% score reduction:
                                             final score = R × m × 0.75

Class Participation grade details: Provide answers to peer questions; ask questions; collaborate on past exams.
We will use forum summary stats (“views,” “contributions,” “questions,” “answers”). Only meaningful contributions
will be considered; students should avoid spamming the forum with comments like “thank you”, “great job”, or
“nice” unnecessarily.
Paper summaries: Sign up on the Wiki and choose two papers from the reading list to summarize (≈1 page each).
If you choose a partial reading to summarize, read and summarize the entire paper.
Sign-up link: CS6210 OMSCS Fall 26 Paper Summary Sign Up


Exam Administration
  • Tests are proctored with Honorlock.

  • You may use one sheet of blank scratch paper. Show both sides to the webcam before starting.
  • 80% of questions are released during the exam window. You may collaborate beforehand, but the exam itself
    is timed, closed-notes, individual, and proctored.


Extra Credit

  • Video Hangout attendance: 0.5% for at least 10 appearances (attendance via PointSolutions quizzes;
    quizzes are ungraded and for attendance/comprehension).

  • Alternative (time-zone constraints): 0.5% via an extra-credit assignment:
        – Summarize any 10 hangout recordings, ≤1 page each.
        – Submit as a single PDF.
        – Eligible only if you cannot attend hangouts and mark attendance via Turning Point.

  • CIOS bonus: If final CIOS completion rate > 85%, everyone receives +0.5% to course total.

  • Putting your projects on public Github.
  • You are encouraged to discuss problems and papers with others as long as this does not involve copying of
    code or solutions. Any public material that you use (AI tools, open-source software, help from a textbook, or

    have any doubt about whether something is legal or not please do check with the class Instructor or the TA.

Zero tolerance for any infraction. It pains me to say this but there have been incidents of infractions in previous
semesters and the consequence is very serious and will affect your academic standing severely. So please do not put
yourself (or me) in that situation.


Collaboration Policy
In order to facilitate a collaborative learning environment, you may discuss the projects and different approaches
and techniques, as well as problems you may be having with each other. However, all the code you turn in must
be the result of your individual effort (including prompts to AI tools). For written homework, similarly,
you may discuss the problems and concepts, but the actual answer that you submit must be written entirely by you.
Please note that any infractions will be reported to the Office of Student Integrity (OSI) and could lead to suspension
from the OMS program.


Late Policy
  • Late submissions accepted for 24 hours after a deadline with a 20% penalty.
  • One Time Forgiveness (OTF):

        – Each student has one OTF during the semester, usable on any graded work except exams.
        – An OTF allows submission up to 1 week past the posted deadline with no penalty.
        – Late submissions are accepted for 24 hours after the OTF deadline (i.e., 8 days after the original deadline)
          with a 20% penalty.
        – To use OTF, complete the OTF request form here: CS 6210 OTF Form. Please check your spam or junk
          folder for the confirmation email.
        – If you face any issue, please create a private Ed post.
